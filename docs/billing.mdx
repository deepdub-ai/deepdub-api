## Credits

All Deepdub usage is measured in credits. Credits are consumed differently depending on whether you use the public model or a private (dedicated) model with a concurrency package.

## Public model billing

On the public model, you pay only for what you generate. Usage is measured in generated audio duration and converted to credits.

There is no reservation cost. If you generate nothing, you pay nothing.

## Concurrency packages

A concurrency package reserves guaranteed capacity for your account. You pay for that reservation whether or not you use it.

The formula is:

```
1,000 credits × available minutes per day × concurrency units = daily cost
```

**Example:** A `concurrency=1` reservation, available 24 hours a day, costs `1,000 × 1,440 × 1 = 1,440,000 credits/day` — regardless of whether you make zero requests or thousands. `concurrency=2` doubles it.

<Warning>
  If you see 1,440,000 (or a multiple) on your dashboard, this is your **capacity reservation cost** — not audio you generated. Concurrency billing is constant per day.
</Warning>

Key facts:
- **1 concurrency unit supports ~4–6 simultaneous calls** — generation runs 3–5× faster than real-time, so one unit handles multiple callers at once.
- **Time-to-first-audio:** typically 100–300 ms on a private model.
- **Provisioning time:** spinning up new concurrency capacity takes ~8–10 minutes. Provision ahead of expected peaks.

For further detail see [Core Concepts: Concurrency and billing](/concepts#concurrency-and-billing).

## Reading your usage dashboard

The dashboard updates once per day at 12:00 AM UTC. Same-day numbers appear after rollover.

<Info>
  If you see a large number on your dashboard and you haven't generated much audio, it is most likely your concurrency reservation cost — not audio you generated. Concurrency billing is constant and appears as a fixed daily charge.
</Info>

For how concurrency actually works under the hood — why one slot can serve several live calls, how to size and pre-warm capacity, and the public-vs-dedicated decision — see [Concurrency & Capacity](/concurrency).
