## Credits

All Deepdub usage is measured in credits. Credits are consumed differently depending on whether you use the public model or a private (dedicated) model with a concurrency package.

## Public model billing

On the public model you pay only for what you generate. Usage is measured in generated audio duration:

- **Rate:** 60 seconds of generated audio = 1,000 credits.

There is no reservation cost. If you generate nothing, you pay nothing. Rate limits on the public queue depend on your subscription package — larger packages allow more concurrent in-flight requests.

## Concurrency packages (dedicated model)

A concurrency package reserves guaranteed capacity for your account. You pay for the reservation whether or not you use it.

- **Rate:** 1,000 credits per slot-minute. So `1,000 × minutes-reserved × concurrency units = total cost`.

<Warning>
  If you see large constant daily numbers on your dashboard, this is your **capacity reservation cost** — not audio you generated. Concurrency billing is constant per day at your fixed reservation.
</Warning>

For worked examples, sizing guidance, and the hybrid public+dedicated pattern, see [Concurrency & Capacity](/concurrency).

## Reading your usage dashboard

The dashboard updates once per day at 12:00 AM UTC. Same-day numbers appear after rollover.

<Info>
  If you see a large number on your dashboard and you haven't generated much audio, it is most likely your concurrency reservation cost — not audio you generated.
</Info>

## Pricing

Per-credit pricing is shown inside your subscription page once you sign in to the [Deepdub dashboard](https://app.deepdub.ai).

## Questions and plan changes

Contact your Deepdub representative to top up credits, adjust your concurrency package, or ask about enterprise plans.
